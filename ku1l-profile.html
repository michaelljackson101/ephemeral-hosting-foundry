🎙️ Welcome & Station Overview

Welcome to the official KU1L station portal and Radio-Foundry hub. I began my amateur radio journey in 2024, earning Technician in February 2024, General in September 2024, and Amateur Extra in May 2025.

I split operating time between our primary shack in Carlisle, MA and our outpost at the Kentucky Farm (Calloway County, KY), frequently traveling the I-90 / Ohio corridor.

⚡ Radio-Foundry Automation & Control Plane

Operating two remote shacks 800 miles apart led to the creation of Radio-Foundry—a modern Linux-native "Radio-as-Code" control plane:

  • Tailscale Encrypted Mesh: Sub-100ms bi-directional Opus audio and CAT rig control over headless Linux edge nodes.
  • Winlink & Digital Modes: Headless Pat Winlink daemon with automated CAT dial tuning and WSJT-X (FT8) with Chrony sub-microsecond precision.
  • Live NetLogger Integration: Automated active net tracking with 1-click transceiver tuning.
  • Smart Power & Protection: Shelly In-Wall relays, Sonoff S31 power monitoring, and automated Paradan antenna grounding.
